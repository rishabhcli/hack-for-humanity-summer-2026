This is not a diagnostic tool. It does not detect, diagnose, rule out or grade whiplash, concussion, or any other condition, and it is not a substitute for assessment by a clinician. Joint position error is a recognised research and clinical measure of cervical proprioception, and repositioning error is reported as elevated in whiplash-associated disorder, chronic neck pain and after concussion. That is a statement about the clinical test, not about this webcam implementation, which has not been validated against instrumented laboratory measurement or in any patient population. Use it to watch your own numbers over time, and take anything that concerns you to a professional. Stop if any movement causes pain or dizziness, and perform the test seated.

What the numbers below are. There is no motion-capture ground truth here. The sweep takes MediaPipe's canonical face mesh, applies a rotation we chose, projects it through a pinhole camera with per-landmark Gaussian noise, and feeds the result to the same estimatePose() the live camera uses. The error is recovered angle minus imposed angle. It measures the estimator's geometry and noise propagation — not MediaPipe's landmark error on real skin, and not this tool's validity against a clinical instrument.
